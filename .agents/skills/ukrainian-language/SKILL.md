# Ukrainian Language Standard

Write natural, accurate, clear, concise, and age-appropriate Ukrainian.

## Language rules

- Follow the current Ukrainian orthography, grammar, and punctuation.
- Use established Ukrainian educational and subject-specific terminology.
- Use one consistent term per concept.
- Prefer short sentences and simple grammatical structures.
- Express one main idea per sentence and one action per instruction.
- Prefer active voice and direct verbs over nominalizations.
- Follow natural Ukrainian syntax and idiomatic usage.
- Avoid literal translations, calques, Russisms, and unnecessary anglicisms.
- Avoid bureaucratic, overly formal, and artificial language.
- Avoid redundancy, ambiguity, and unnecessary synonyms.
- Make conditions, actions, and references explicit.
- Maintain consistent tone, terminology, and forms of address.
- Preserve placeholders, formulas, markup, and identifiers.

## Educational rules

- Adapt vocabulary and sentence complexity to the student's grade level.
- Preserve factual, mathematical, and pedagogical accuracy.
- Explain reasoning without skipping essential logical steps.
- Use precise subject-specific terminology instead of artificial simplifications.
- Avoid patronizing language and subjective judgments of difficulty.
- Provide constructive, actionable feedback.

## Preferred usage

- «правильна відповідь» instead of «вірна відповідь»
- «розв'язати задачу» instead of «вирішити задачу»
- «наступний» instead of «слідуючий»
- «цей» instead of «даний» when referring to an object
- «є» instead of «являється»
- «під час» instead of «в ході»
- «за замовчуванням» instead of «по замовчуванню»
- «застосунок» instead of «аплікація»
- «обчисли» instead of «здійсни обчислення»
- «перевір» instead of «виконай перевірку»
- «вибери» instead of «здійсни вибір»
- «якщо» instead of «у випадку, якщо»
- «щоб» instead of «з метою»

Apply these preferences contextually, not as mechanical replacements.

## Validation

Before finalizing, verify:

1. Semantic, factual, and subject-specific accuracy.
2. Orthographic, grammatical, and syntactic correctness.
3. Naturalness and absence of linguistic calques.
4. Terminological and stylistic consistency.
5. Clarity, conciseness, and age appropriateness.

Simplify language, not educational meaning.
Never sacrifice correctness for simplicity.
